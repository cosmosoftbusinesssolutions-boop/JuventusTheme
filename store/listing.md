## Full description (max 4000 characters)
Give your TV a home screen that looks as good as what you watch.

TV Theme replaces your TV's home screen with a bold, clean design in black, white and gold. It's built for the
remote: everything is one or two clicks away, and there are no ads.

★ A HOME SCREEN THAT'S YOURS
• Pin your favorite apps to the top and put them in any order
• Hide the apps you never use
• Turn whole rows on or off
• Striped Bianconeri wallpaper, three more built-in wallpapers, color themes, or your own picture. Picture
  wallpapers recolor the whole launcher to match.

★ PLEX BUILT IN
• Continue watching, your watchlist and "Recently added" rows on the home screen
• Movie and show pages with seasons, episodes, cast and ratings
• Resume where you left off and mark things watched
• Sign in safely with a code at plex.tv/link. Your password is never typed on the TV.

★ PROFILES FOR THE WHOLE FAMILY
• A profile for everyone, each with its own apps, rows and wallpaper
• Lock any profile with a 4-digit PIN
• Kids profiles: choose which apps they see, limit Plex titles by age rating, and keep settings locked

★ QUICK SETTINGS
• Network status, volume and shortcuts to the TV settings you use most, in a panel on the side

FREE FOR 14 DAYS
Try everything free for 14 days. Then keep Premium (Plex, picture wallpapers and profiles) with a one-time
lifetime purchase or a low monthly subscription. The home screen itself keeps working either way.

REQUIREMENTS
Android TV or Google TV running Android 8.0 or newer. On Google TV devices, the system doesn't let you replace the
home screen from Settings. Open TV Theme from your app list, or follow the in-app guide to make it your home screen.
