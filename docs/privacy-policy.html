TV Theme – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

TV Theme ("the app") is a home screen (launcher) for Android TV and Google TV, published by [DEVELOPER NAME]. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes. In short: we do not collect, sell or share your personal data, and we do not run any servers that receive it.

Information stored on your TV

These settings stay on your TV and are deleted when you uninstall the app:

  • Your favorites, hidden apps, home screen rows and wallpaper choice for each profile.
  • Profile names and PINs. PINs are stored only as a one-way hash, never in readable form.
  • If you choose your own picture as wallpaper, a resized copy of that picture.
  • If you connect Plex, the sign-in token Plex issues and the name of the server you chose.

The app turns off Android backup, so none of this is copied to cloud backups.

The list of installed apps

As a home screen, the app reads the list of apps installed on your TV so it can show and open them. This list is only used on the TV and is never sent anywhere.

Plex (optional)

If you choose to connect a Plex account, the app talks directly to Plex: to plex.tv to sign you in and find your servers, to Plex's catalog service for your watchlist, and to your own Plex Media Server for your libraries, artwork, playback and watch progress. You sign in on plex.tv with a short code, so the app never sees your Plex password. This data goes only between your TV and Plex and is governed by Plex's privacy policy. You can disconnect any time in Customize → Plex → Sign out.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. The app only learns whether you own TV Theme Premium. It never sees your payment details. See Google's privacy policy.

Photos

The app only gets access to a picture you pick yourself as a wallpaper, through Android's photo picker. It cannot see your other photos.

Analytics and advertising

The app contains no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Children

The app is not directed at children. Parents can create restricted profiles that limit which apps and which Plex content ratings appear. These controls work entirely on the TV.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
