Install on your TV

The easiest way is the free Downloader app (by AFTVnews), available on the Play Store and the Amazon Appstore.

  1. On your TV, install and open Downloader.
  2. Type this code:
    [DOWNLOADER CODE]
    or the full link: https://github.com/cosmosoftbusinesssolutions-boop/JuventusTheme/releases/latest/download/TVTheme.apk
  3. When the TV asks, allow Downloader to install apps, then press Install.
  4. Press Home on your remote. If the TV asks which home app to use, choose TV Theme and Always.

On Google TV and Fire TV, which don't let you change the home app: open TV Theme from your app list, then go to Quick settings → Make TV Theme your home screen and turn on the Home button helper.

Prefer a USB stick? Download the file on a computer, copy it to a USB stick, and open it on the TV with a file manager such as File Commander or X-plore.

Works on

TVs and boxes running Android TV or Google TV, Android 8.0 or newer: Sony, TCL, Philips, Xiaomi, Android models from Hisense, Chromecast with Google TV, NVIDIA Shield, Mi Box and similar, plus Amazon Fire TV. Not for Samsung (Tizen), LG (webOS) or Roku TVs, which can't run Android apps.
